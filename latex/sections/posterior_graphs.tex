\subsubsection{Interpreting the fitted estimates}
For jute, unrestricted MLE reliability is about 0.7526 and the posterior mean about 0.6656. Bayes averages reliability over the posterior, rather than evaluating it at the mean rates. The ordered posterior mean is about 0.6989, partly reflecting exclusion of reliability below one half. For the carbon split, the unrestricted and ordered posterior means are about 0.3623 and 0.5653. That difference is sensitivity to an assumption, rather than automatic improvement.
Only thirteen failures and six stress records enter the jute likelihood, so the wide uncertainty should be retained. Parameter posterior histograms and chain traces remain in the executed notebook; the report gives their numerical diagnostics.
