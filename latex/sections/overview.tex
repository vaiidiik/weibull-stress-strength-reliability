\subsection{Project outline and notation}
We derive the reliability and likelihood, fit unrestricted and ordered models, compare their errors in simulation, and examine published measurements. The raw measurements, retrospective retained observations and simulated data are saved separately. Population truths are known only in simulation; observed-data MSE is therefore not reported for the applications.
