\section{Sources and reproducibility}\label{sec:appendix-sources}
Sultana et al.\ \cite{Sultana2026} provide the block Weibull framework, order restriction and dependent prior, and reproduce the jute measurements. Newer \cite{Newer2025} provides the combined censoring/record framework for another distribution. Newer and Alanazi \cite{NewerAlanazi2026} provide related reliability and bootstrap ideas and the reproduced carbon table. We derive the assigned Weibull model separately. The primary gauge workbooks are from the authors' Zenodo deposit \cite{KumarData2020}, linked by Kumar et al.\ \cite{Kumar2024}.

The other supplied hybrid-inference paper informs interval reporting. The supplied attention-based control-chart paper is outside this model, so its trading data and machine-learning methods are not used. For the assigned SAGE reference, this work used the publisher abstract and citation details; full text was not accessed in this session. Source-by-source details are in \path{code/notes/literature_review.md}.

Full measurement listings, retained observations and the 48-row simulation design are stored in \path{data/}, rather than repeated in the report. The executed notebook and statistical scripts are in \path{code/}; all additional plots remain in \path{graphs/}. The primary workbook filenames, row rule, checksums and units are recorded in \path{data/raw/primary_carbon_provenance.json}. Seeds and priors are fixed in the code. The README gives execution and compilation commands.
