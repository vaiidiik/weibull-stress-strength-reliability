\section{Source use and reproducibility}\label{sec:appendix-sources}
The Weibull block paper \cite{Sultana2026} supplies the relevant common-shape parameterization, order-restricted inference and gamma-Dirichlet prior construction. Its reproduced jute table is our transcription source. The Scientific Reports paper \cite{Newer2025} supplies the combined censoring/record framework for a different distribution. We derive the Weibull reliability and likelihood separately. The Journal of Mathematics paper \cite{NewerAlanazi2026} gives related conditional reliability and bootstrap ideas and supplies the reproduced carbon measurements.

Two other supplied articles were reviewed. The generalized inverted exponential paper by Hasaballah and coauthors concerns unified hybrid inference and provides background on interval reporting; its distribution and COVID-19 data are not used here. The attention-based ratio control-chart article by Yeganeh and coauthors is outside the assigned reliability problem, so none of its trading data or machine-learning procedures is imported. The assigned SAGE reference was available only at abstract level. Its full text was not supplied, and no derivation from its unavailable full text is claimed. The detailed source-use notes remain in \path{code/notes/literature_review.md}.

The repository separates raw measurements, retained observations and computed results. The notebook in \path{code/} contains saved tables and graphs. \path{code/analysis.py} contains the statistical functions; the other scripts execute the notebook and prepare the report tables and graphs. The complete simulation can be repeated by enabling its stated run switch. The master seed and scenario-specific streams are fixed. The LaTeX sources and bibliography are in \path{latex/}, and every graph is in \path{graphs/}.

The main results do not depend on reading the locally archived original article PDFs at runtime. Those documents remain available locally for source checking, but are not part of the public repository. This separation keeps the submitted project focused on the report, code, data and computed evidence.
