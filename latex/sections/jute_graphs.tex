\subsubsection{Jute fit and retained-data graphs}
The density plots compare histograms of the complete measurements with fitted Weibull densities. Histogram shape depends on binning, so a visual match alone is not a test. The CDF panels compare all ordered observations with fitted cumulative probabilities. The Q-Q panels show where sample quantiles differ from those predicted by the fitted model.

These complete-data checks use every measurement in the two groups. They are separate from the later retained-data likelihood. The failure plot below shows only observed failures, with ring size indicating the actual number withdrawn at a failure.
