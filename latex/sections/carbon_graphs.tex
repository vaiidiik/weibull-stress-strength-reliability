\subsubsection{Carbon fit graphs}
The carbon figures repeat the same density, CDF and Q-Q checks for disjoint groups obtained by the seeded split. Both groups measure the same physical quantity, and both are scaled by the fixed 3 GPa unit before inference. This keeps the comparison meaningful in units, although one group is still a reference stress population rather than measured operating loads.

\pairplot{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_01.png}{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_04.png}{Carbon histograms and fitted Weibull densities for the strength and comparison halves, in units of 3 GPa.}{fig:carbon-density}
\pairplot{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_02.png}{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_05.png}{Carbon empirical and fitted cumulative distributions for the two allocated halves.}{fig:carbon-cdf}
\pairplot{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_03.png}{12_carbon_data_fit_panel_06.png}{Carbon Weibull Q-Q plots for the two allocated halves. The calibrated tests are compatible with the fitted model at the stated level.}{fig:carbon-qq}
The closer visual agreement and the calibrated tests support this additional illustration. They do not establish that the fitted distribution is exact or that future experiments must behave the same way. The estimates below are conditional on common shape, independent observations before selection and noninformative withdrawals.
